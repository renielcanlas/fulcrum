# Regulatory research plan

This is a research plan, not regulatory advice or a finding. Sources must be primary, current, jurisdiction-appropriate, and reviewed by FCRM/legal. Candidate authorities: FATF risk-based guidance; FinCEN BSA/AML and CDD materials; OCC, Federal Reserve, FDIC, and FFIEC supervisory guidance. Record source URL, publication/update date, scope, quoted locator, interpretation, reviewer, and effective version in policy references.

Questions: which sources govern the target bank and products; what risk dimensions are expected; what evidence and control expectations apply; what governance/record retention requirements apply; and what claims may be encoded as rules? Do not convert a source into a scoring rule without owner approval and a configuration version.
