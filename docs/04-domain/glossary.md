# Domain glossary

**Change request / initiative:** proposed product, feature, process, vendor, geography, or customer-segment change. **Inherent risk:** exposure before considering controls. **Control environment:** relevant preventive/detective controls and their assessed design/operation. **Residual risk:** remaining exposure after controls. **Evidence quality:** source reliability, completeness, freshness, and traceability. **Assessment completeness:** required information and analysis coverage. **Risk factor:** modeled dimension contributing to exposure. **Finding:** evidence-backed observation requiring consideration/action. **Recommendation:** analyst or AI proposal, not a decision. **Override:** authorized human departure from an AI suggestion or configured result, with rationale. **Decision:** committee’s authorized outcome. **Policy reference:** citation to source material plus interpretation boundary. **Decision-ready:** required evidence, analysis, review, and unresolved items are explicitly represented.
