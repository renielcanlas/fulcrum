# Operations strategy

Observe workflow throughput and stuck cases, queue health, error/latency, provider availability, retrieval quality, schema failures, grounding/citation failures, human overrides, audit completeness, token/cost usage, and configuration changes. Establish runbooks for provider outage, bad retrieval, malicious upload, unauthorized access, scoring misconfiguration, data restoration, and incident escalation.

AI may summarize incidents and detect anomalies as advisory outputs. Operators retain authority to pause workflows, revoke integrations, roll back models/prompts/configuration, and notify stakeholders. Drift and feedback reviews feed controlled evaluation dataset updates; no silent online learning.
