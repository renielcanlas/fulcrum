# Assumptions, open questions, and research-required items

## Assumptions

- Hackathon cases and documents are synthetic.
- FCRM owns taxonomy, parameter configuration, and analyst/committee role definitions.
- Existing enterprise identity, document storage, and policy repositories may be represented by demo adapters.
- A modular monolith with worker jobs is sufficient for the demonstration.

## Open questions

- Which business-unit products, jurisdictions, and change types are in the minimum viable demo?
- Which authoritative internal policies and retention/classification rules apply?
- What are baseline turnaround, analyst effort, and acceptable false-positive/false-negative tradeoffs?
- Who may configure parameters, publish versions, and approve exceptions?
- What committee quorum, conditions, deferral, and re-assessment rules are required?
- What retention, legal hold, residency, encryption, and identity standards apply?
- Which integrations are real versus demo-only?

## Research required

Confirm the selected supervisory framework, jurisdictional applicability, risk-factor taxonomy, recordkeeping expectations, model-risk expectations, and policy ownership with qualified FCRM/legal/compliance stakeholders. No regulatory requirement is asserted in this bootstrap. Research plan: [regulatory-research-plan.md](../02-research/regulatory-research-plan.md).
