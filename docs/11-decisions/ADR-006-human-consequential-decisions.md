# ADR-006: Human-controlled consequential decisions

Status: Accepted. Requirements: REQ-005, REQ-007, REQ-008.

## Context

Risk disposition and committee outcomes are consequential governance actions.

## Decision

AI may prepare, explain, challenge, and recommend. Analysts control interpretation, readiness, and overrides. Authorized committee members control final disposition. The system enforces gates and records rationale; no AI or automation can finalize a decision.

## Consequences

Human review remains a required throughput step, so UX must make evidence and disagreements efficient. Quality metrics include review coverage, override quality, and decision traceability—not just automation rate.
