# AI policy boundaries

Allowed: scoped retrieval, extraction, normalization, gap detection, classification assistance, drafting, challenge, and read-only Q&A. Prohibited: final approval/rejection, unauthorized state/configuration mutation, authorization bypass, fabricated or uncited evidence, concealment of uncertainty, and treating conversation memory as authority. Every material output is attributable, explainable, evidence-backed, reviewable, overridable, and auditable.
